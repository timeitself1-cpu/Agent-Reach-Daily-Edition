# Agent Reach frontend audit and improvement plan — round 2

Reviewed October 8, 2026 against `main` at `90e605c` (the October 8 edition, revision 2: 53 stories). Round 1 is recorded in [frontend-audit.md](frontend-audit.md). Its five priorities (mobile navigation, search recovery, keyboard continuity, editorial hierarchy, and evidence/edition context) are complete. This round checks the site as it now ships, with a second day of real data, and plans the next set of improvements. The follow-up roadmap, reviewed against revision 6 and the app's HTML export, is in [roadmap.md](roadmap.md).

## How this was checked, and its limits

- **Live site.** https://getagentreach.dev/ could not be reached from the audit environment: the outbound proxy rejected the connection, and the fetch service could not resolve the host. The site has no build step, and Cloudflare serves the repository root as committed (`wrangler.jsonc`, `assets.directory: "."`). So this audit served commit `90e605c` locally and tested that. Some items depend on the live host (response headers, compression, which files are published) and are marked **verify live** below.
- **Browser.** Chromium (Playwright 1.56) on nine views: Home, Latest, Technology, World, Archive, Search (`?q=openai`), About, a story page, and the archived October 7 edition. Each was checked at 320, 390, 768 and 1440px. axe-core 4.10 ran with the WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 A/AA and best-practice rules at 390 and 1440px. Load timing used a simulated slow-4G connection (150ms latency, 1.6Mbps down). Print output was checked with Chromium's PDF renderer.
- **DOM tests.** `tests/frontend.cjs` was run with Node 22 and jsdom 26, which stand in for the documented Node 24 and jsdom 30.1.2.
- No fixtures, shells or publishing files were changed.

## What is holding up well

- No horizontal overflow on any of the 36 view/width combinations. All seven section links stay visible.
- axe reports **no critical or serious violations** on any view. Only two moderate best-practice rules fire (see minor findings).
- Secondary text colours measure at least 5.89:1 against every surface. Text is always inserted as plain text, and reduced-motion support is in place.
- Search, recovery, focus handling and edition context from round 1 behave as documented.

## Minor findings

- **axe, moderate.** `region`: the skip link sits outside any landmark (16 of 18 view/width runs). `landmark-complementary-is-top-level`: the story `aside` is inside `main`. Fixes: put the skip link in a top `nav`/`header` context, and turn the story aside into a `section` with a heading.
- **Archive rows.** Each whole row is one link, so its accessible name is the day, the lead, three headlines, the section chips and the count, read in one run. Make the lead headline the link and stretch its click target over the row instead.
- **Inconsistent timestamps.** Source times inside card disclosures use `stamp()`, which has no year and no time zone. The story page uses the full form. Relative times ("40 min ago") are computed once and never refresh in a tab left open.
- **404 wording.** Any never-published `/daily/YYYY-MM-DD/`, including a mistyped date, says "This edition was taken off the site". Use "This edition isn't available. It may have been withdrawn." and keep the archive link.
- **Small targets.** "Read the story and its sources" and the band "All …" links are 22px tall. They pass WCAG 2.5.8 through spacing, but fall short of the site's own 44px convention.
- **Heading order.** On story pages the footer's `h3` headings follow the aside's `h3` items, with no `h2` above them.
- **Search layout shift.** CLS is 0.063 on `/search/?q=…` as the status line fills. Reserve one line of height.
- **Data mismatches to watch with the publisher.** In today's edition, 37 of 53 stories (70%) are single-source and 1 of 53 has a "why it matters" note. The lead and story layouts still reserve visual space for "Why it matters", which almost never renders; that is fine as built. A "strong coverage only" view could be worth testing later.

## Review sequence

Each step is a reviewable pull request. The step 1 harness should land before or with any later change.

1. Hermetic test fixtures, contract smoke test, and CI workflow (priority 1).
2. Band "Show all" expansion and concurrent edition loading with an Inter preload (priorities 2 and 3, frontend part). Record LCP before and after with the same throttling profile.
3. Share / Copy link and `og:image` on frontend-owned shells (priority 4, frontend part).
4. Compact story-page edition line and print stylesheet (priorities 5 and 6).
5. `.assetsignore`, inline styles moved to classes, then `_headers` (priority 7), after the live check.
6. Minor findings, grouped into one accessibility/consistency pull request.

Publisher items to raise in [backend-coordination.md](backend-coordination.md): server-rendered `#app` content (priority 3), `og:image`/`twitter:card` on dated shells (priority 4), and sitemap entries if more section routes are added (priority 2). Per-story URLs and per-story RSS items still depend on stable event ids.

## Preserve

The publishing contract and JSON fields, monthly search files and their batching, URLs (including `#story-` addresses and the `headline` hint), RSS/sitemap/robots, source grouping and counts, the coverage method, edition/revision/freshness indicators, plain-text rendering, self-hosted fonts, the no-tracking promise, and reduced-motion support. This plan adds no runtime dependency, analytics or third-party request.
